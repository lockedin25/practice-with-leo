# Meridian reservoir validation contract

This is a qualitative-conversation prototype. It intentionally does not expose
calculation exercises, even though source economics remain in `facts.yaml` as
private consistency evidence.

## Diagnosis preservation

The following proposition must remain true in every playable reservoir version:

> Margin deterioration is caused by a shift into pharma work whose lower-
> utilisation cost-to-serve was not reflected in the contract price.

No synthetic fact may introduce a different primary cause, a contradictory
segment definition, a regional explanation, or a recommendation that assumes
food and pharma loads can simply be consolidated.

## Qualitative augmentation checks

Before the reservoir becomes playable, verify that each synthetic fact:

1. Gives useful business context or tests a realistic assumption.
2. Does not contain a new numerical claim.
3. Does not reveal the diagnosis or shortcut a pivotal question.
4. Does not conflict with a source fact, source timeline, or hidden diagnosis.
5. Has a legitimate release level and candidate purpose.

## Conversation checks

Simulate at least these routes:

| Route | Candidate path | Expected interviewer behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Broad opener | Asks what Meridian does and how it makes money | Gives company and business-model context, then asks for a structure. |
| Segment route | Tests mix, then segment profitability | Answers the sharp mix question and lets the candidate pursue the logic. |
| Geography detour | Asks about regional performance | Answers broadly, permits one follow-up, then redirects toward route economics. |
| Weak structure | Gives a generic revenue-cost framework | Challenges the lack of prioritisation without supplying the issue tree. |
| Premature answer | Claims pharma is the cause without evidence | Asks what economic mechanism would make the segment unattractive. |
| Synthesis | Recommends repricing and better route planning | Tests service trade-offs and asks for implementation risk. |

## Runtime boundary

For V1, the conversational runtime may select only existing facts from this
snapshot. The LLM must generate each interviewer response from the transcript
and approved facts; no response templates, nudges, or stored wording may be
used. It may not create a fact, alter a fact, or disclose `interviewer_only`
content.
