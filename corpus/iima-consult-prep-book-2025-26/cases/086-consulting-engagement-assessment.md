I see three factors: Technological Complexity, Scale of Operations and Geographical Distribution. I would rate the first two factors to be much more complex and nuanced in the mining sector, hence transferring skills to the retail sector would not be an issue. So, we might face challenges on the third factor - optimizing thousands of distributed stores. Coming to Resources Available, do we have enough quantum as well as skill levels of human resources (consultants) available to undertake new projects?

### Turn 14 - Interviewer

Yes, there is no shortage of skilled consultants at our firm in the foreseeable future. We can move on.

### Turn 15 - Candidate
