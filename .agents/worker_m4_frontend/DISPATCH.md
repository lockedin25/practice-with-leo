## Required 7 Cards & Features
1. **Executive Header Card**: Overall score (out of 5.0), MBB rating badge (*Distinction*, *Strong Pass*, *Meets Bar*, *Developing*, *Needs Substantial Practice*), and 2-sentence Executive Summary partner assessment.
2. **"What This Case Really Wanted to Test" Masterclass Card**: Explains the core business mechanism, pivotal insight, and how the candidate performed against it.
3. **5 Core Dimension Cards**: 1.0–5.0 score bars, band labels, verbatim evidence blockquotes, and observation notes across `structuring`, `problem_solving`, `quantitative`, `synthesis`, `communication`.
4. **Golden MBB Rewrites Comparison**: Side-by-side comparison for Opening/Clarification and Closing Synthesis (Candidate Turn vs Top 1% MBB Partner Turn) with diagnostic notes.
5. **Strengths & Areas for Development**: Split-card layout with verbatim-cited strengths and prioritized actionable development guidance.
6. **Action Plan & Resources**: Next 2-minute drill card and clickable suggested readings from `knowledge_base/`.
7. **Observed Behaviors Checklist**: Interactive checklist of demonstrated vs missed markers with status badges and filters.
